# Delhivery — Go-To-Market Strategy
### Case competition: finalist

**Brief:** Delhivery wants to launch a consumer mobile app that lets individuals and small businesses send parcels from home across India and internationally. Design a go-to-market strategy covering marketing and advertising, branding, differentiation, product features, customer service and partnerships for a pan-India launch.

## Approach

- **Secondary research:** Indian retail and e-commerce market sizing, logistics and courier landscape.
- **Primary research:** a survey with 156 responses, mostly C2C users, on courier choice, shipping frequency and adoption barriers.
- **Competitive analysis:** India Post, Blue Dart, DTDC and Delhivery on a perceptual map of digitization against trust.

## Key insight

Delhivery is strong on digital features like its app, but trust matters most to C2C and small-business senders, and Delhivery trails traditional couriers on trust. That gap explains the limited adoption in this segment. The deck builds its adoption strategy around closing it.
