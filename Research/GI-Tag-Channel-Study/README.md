# When the Law Protects but the Market Does Not
### GI-tagged products: a channel comparison study

Research carried out at **IIT Guwahati** (Operations & Supply Chain Management) under **Dr. Atanu Bhuyan**.

## Scope

A comparison of how authentic Indian products are sold online, covering **5 GI-tagged product categories** across **4 channel types**.

## Central finding

GI registration works as a *legal* instrument but not as a *market* instrument. Some products, such as Jaipur Blue Pottery, hold GI status but have no point-of-sale certification enforcement. Others, such as saffron (NABL, FSSAI) and sarees (Silk Mark), have a certification mechanism that reaches the buyer. Without such a mechanism, a GI tag does little to help a buyer tell authentic products from imitations.
