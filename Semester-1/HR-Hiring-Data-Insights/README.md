# Hiring Driven by Data Insights of the Applicants

**Group project**, Semester 1, MSDSM (IIM Amritsar & IIT Ropar). Course: OB & HRM, under Dr. Neeti Ingole.

## Problem

Manual resume screening is slow and inconsistent, especially at volume. The project asks whether applicant data can predict hiring outcomes and help HR rank candidates.

## Approach

- Exploratory analysis of applicant data and hypothesis testing.
- Preprocessing and feature encoding, with attention to class imbalance.
- Three classifiers compared: **Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest**, evaluated on accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
- Results interpreted from a people-management viewpoint to inform HR practice.
- The report also proposes candidate ranking, skill-based matching and certificate verification as part of a resume-processing application.
