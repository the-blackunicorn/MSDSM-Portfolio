# How BJP Used WhatsApp to Create a Word-of-Mouth Campaign

**Group 4 project**, Marketing Management (MAR402), MSDSM-03, presented on 5 October 2025 to Prof. Sakshi Aggarwal.

## Topic

A marketing case study on **social networks and digital media as a marketing channel**. It looks at how a political campaign used WhatsApp to build a word-of-mouth (WOM) campaign, and what that shows about the shift in marketing style from broadcast media to networks of people.
