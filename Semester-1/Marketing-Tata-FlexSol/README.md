# Tata FlexSol — Power That Bends With You

**Group 4 project**, Marketing Management (MAR402), MSDSM-03, presented to Prof. Sakshi Aggarwal.

## Concept

A marketing plan for a **new-to-India product category** from Tata Power Solar (29.2% market share in 2024): flexible, lightweight **organic photovoltaic (OPV) solar films**, ultra-thin (under 1 mm) and installed by "peel and stick" without frames or drilling.

## Why

Urban rooftops have space and weight limits, and old, curved or temporary structures can't take rigid silicon panels. The plan connects to the shift towards Building-Integrated Photovoltaics and India's Net Zero 2070 goal.

## Covered in the deck

Company background, product concept and technology, strategic intent, segmentation, targeting, positioning, and the full 4Ps marketing mix with a product line of **FlexSol Roof**, **FlexSol Wall** and **FlexSol Move**. The pricing section is built on the high cost of imported OPV films.
