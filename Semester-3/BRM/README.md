# Usage & Attitude Study of UPI Applications in India

**Group 4 project**, Business Research Methods (BRM), Semester 3, instructor Dr. Amar Saxena.

## Research question

What should a UPI app provider focus on to become a user's **primary** UPI app and lower the chance of users switching to other apps? The study looks at what makes people pick a primary app, what makes them switch, how much different features matter, why trusted bank apps and BHIM, and high-reach apps like WhatsApp, are used so little, and whether switching is planned or just habit.

## How the project flows

Management decision problem, then research problem, five research objectives and sixteen research questions, then information areas, then the questionnaire.

| Submission | Covers |
|------------|--------|
| 3 | Research approach and design, a qualitative study, the questionnaire and the analysis plan |
| 4 | Data collection on Qualtrics, data preparation, analysis, findings and what they mean for the research questions |

The questionnaire was reviewed against 15 rules of questionnaire design and corrected. Submission 4 reports results from **simulated data, used with the instructor's approval.**
