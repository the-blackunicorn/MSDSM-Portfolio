# Equifax: Ethical Risks in Big Data, Analytics and AI

**Group 2 project**, Semester 3, M.Sc. Data Science & Management (IIM Amritsar & IIT Ropar). Team: Abhinav Rijit, Abhishek Dewangan, Kesana Sai Krishna, Simran Sanjay Nimje, Vivek Shivhare.

## What it is

A 6-page ethics and governance report on **Equifax**, a credit bureau that holds data on people who never chose to be in its systems. It covers four areas of concern:

- the 2017 breach, which exposed about 147 million people
- the 2022 credit-score coding error
- the risk of opaque machine-learning scoring
- the chance of such failures recurring

## Approach

The failures are analysed with the course frameworks: Five Barriers to ethical action, Moral Disengagement, Right versus Right, ethical theories, mandatory versus voluntary limits, and the Prisoner's Dilemma. The report also notes where each framework falls short.

It closes with **five recommendations**, each with a technical mechanism and a legal anchor under US, EU and Indian law (GDPR, FCRA, ECOA, the DPDP Act and others):

1. Make stolen data useless
2. Hold less data
3. Govern every model
4. Explain and test for fairness
5. Disclose quickly and fix accountability

A 90-day and 12–24-month roadmap sets the order of work.
