# The Driverless Field: AI Is Reshaping John Deere and the Machinery Industry

**Group 4 project**, Strategic Management, Semester 3, M.Sc. Data Science & Management (IIM Amritsar & IIT Ropar).

## What it is

A strategic analysis of **Deere & Company (John Deere)** and its second-generation autonomous tractor and Autonomy Kit, asking how AI is reshaping strategy in agricultural machinery and precision agriculture. The report applies external and internal analysis frameworks and ends with recommendations.

One of the findings in the slides: among the five forces, **buyer power is the one that is changing**. Retrofit alternatives and subscription fatigue give farmers exit options they did not have before.
